# Your Horoscope Is Not Science

A short, kind course that uses horoscopes to teach what makes a claim testable. No ridicule, just the rules for evidence a learner can use anywhere.

- **Topic:** How to tell science from not-science: testability and evidence
- **Grade:** 7
- **Lens:** none
- **License:** CC-BY-4.0
- **Units:** 1  **Lessons:** 2  **Items:** 6
