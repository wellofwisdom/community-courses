# Fractions Through Sewing

Lens course only this app would make. Every fraction is a piece of fabric. Cut, measure, and join: halves, quarters, and eighths on the cutting mat, with seam allowance you can measure.

- **Topic:** Fractions: halves, quarters, eighths and joining them
- **Grade:** 5
- **Lens:** sewing

- **Units:** 1  **Lessons:** 2  **Items:** 7
