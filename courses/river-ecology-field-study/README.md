# River Ecology Field Study

A co-op ready field course. Observe a real creek or river near you: measure water, map life, and make a small care plan. Each lesson has a page for notes and a task to do outside.

- **Topic:** How a river lives: water, food webs, and human care
- **Grade:** 6
- **Lens:** field science
- **License:** CC-BY-4.0
- **Units:** 2  **Lessons:** 4  **Items:** 11
